Notifications or sound not working?

Closed or blocked Chrome’s permission panel? Click the site controls icon beside the address, then open Site settings → Notifications → Allow. Reload this page and enable browser reminders again. A quiet prompt may appear as a blocked-notification icon in the address bar. You do not need to allow pop-up windows.

Allowed, but no banner? On Windows, open Settings → System → Notifications → Google Chrome (or your browser). Check notifications and Show notification banners. Look in the notification centre too. Do not disturb / Focus and automatic rules for games or full-screen apps can hide banners. Choose the settings that suit you.

No sound? Enable Reminder sound and click Test sound. Check the tab/site is not muted, device volume and audio output. This chime is separate from Windows’ notification sound and needs enabling each visit.

Test works, but an event does not notify? Select Remind on that event and check reminders and lead time. Done today suppresses reminders until daily reset. Unverified schedules cannot notify. Keep the tab open and computer awake; suspended tabs may miss or delay alerts. Use one active reminder tab.

Try a regular desktop Chrome or Edge window if an embedded or private browser does not work. “Test requested” cannot confirm that a banner appeared or sound was heard.
